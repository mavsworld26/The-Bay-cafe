Our Story

A local table, right on the water

The Bay Café sits on the Mandurah foreshore, where the estuary laps at the decking and the boats drift past on their way out for a morning cruise. We're a genuinely local spot — the kind of café where regulars get a nod, dogs get a bowl of water, and the birthday table gets a bit of extra fuss.

Our kitchen keeps it honest: hearty all-day breakfasts, fresh salads and seafood, house-made cakes, and coffee that's worth the occasional queue. Order at the counter, grab a table inside or out on the deck, and let the view do the rest.

  • Fresh, generous, café-classic plates
  • Dog-friendly outdoor seating
  • Gluten-free options available
  • Front-row estuary & dolphin views
